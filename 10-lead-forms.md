# Lead Forms

## General inquiry form (homepage + service pages)

- Name
- Company
- Business Email
- Role
- What do you need? *(dropdown)*
  - Cloud Migration
  - DevOps
  - Kubernetes / OpenShift
  - SRE
  - Infrastructure Operations
  - VMware Modernization
  - Staff Augmentation
  - Other
- Technology Environment *(free text)*
- Number of Engineers Required *(dropdown)*
  - 1
  - 2–3
  - 4–6
  - 7+
- Engagement Type *(dropdown)*
  - Project
  - Short-term
  - Long-term
  - Not sure
- Tell us about your requirements *(free text)*
- Submit

## Staff Augmentation inquiry form (dedicated version for `/devops-staff-augmentation`)

Same fields as above, pre-filtered so "What do you need?" defaults to Staff Augmentation, plus:

- Preferred start date
- Time-zone overlap requirement
- Preferred engagement model *(dropdown: Dedicated Engineer / Engineering Pod / Project-Based / Long-Term Support)*

## Notes for developer handoff
- Route submissions by "What do you need?" value so Staff Augmentation leads reach sales/staffing contacts and Project leads reach delivery/solutions contacts.
- Confirmation email copy should restate the three conversion paths from the homepage so leads know what happens next.
